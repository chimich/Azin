Design 2

KEYWORDS: Vernacular Architecture, Sustainable Landscape Architecture
SITE: Tehran, Iran
Spring 2022 - Professor Vatandoost

What was considered in the design process was how to keep a share of the yard and open and semi-open space for the upper unit. So that the qualities of a villa house (open, semi-open, closed structure) on the upper floor of the building will still be maintained and the complex will be a combination of two complete houses, not a modern apartment. On the other hand, due to the presence of vast green space in the east of the site, inspired by the basic elements of Iranian architecture, a "balcony for viewing" was formed so that one can watch the city through it.

Therefore, various areas (open, semi-open) such as the entrance yard (porch), upper yard, moonlight and roof were formed, which in combination with closed spaces in a purposeful manner and with each other through certain rituals, the possibility of various activities and behaviors such as Children's play, viewing the right scenery, reading, solitude and being together, cooking, eating and sleeping under the night sky provide more quality and comfort.
