Final Project

KEYWORDS: Desert, Night, Silence, Resort Hotel, Solitude
SITE: Shahdad, Kerman, Iran
Spring 2023 - Professor Ramezani

The desert is an important and vast landscape on earth, which covers about one third of the land, and apart from its natural landscape, its unpolluted and organic atmosphere, peace and silence, confusion, wonder, loneliness, and infinite vision are among its unique features, as if it makes humans in Facing oneself drowns in the fantasy of existence. The desert has always been a source of inspiration, and its silence and vastness have mesmerized man and encouraged him to move beyond space and place and step into the land of dreams, because nothing but fantasy lives in the desert. The desert is the embodiment of imagination. Taking refuge in the desert is to fall into the trap of liberation because a moment of lingering in it plunges a person into the depth of thought and the feeling of being pervades his whole being. Man needs to be invited to be among this space polo. An inviter of architecture to teach him in the moment. The mother of a lost person, like his ancestors, is a refuge from the soil of nature, which in the middle of the exhausting ways of life or even the desert, gives him the opportunity to rest and pause, to catch his breath and watch the fantasy. Shelter or residence is the lost thing that we are looking for.

Considering the different perspectives of Shahdad desert, it was tried to facilitate the understanding of the space with architecture, so that the viewpoints that are framed by the architecture are presented to the visitor. It was tried to emphasize the presence of nature and strengthen its sense by creating forms consistent with the landscape.
