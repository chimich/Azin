Hi, I am Azin Kamangar

I’m Azin Kamangar, a Bachelor of Architecture graduate from Pars University of Architecture and Art. My work centers on contextual architecture, narrative architecture, and the philosophy of place-making, with a deep commitment to honoring the spirit and quality of place. Through my designs, I strive to create harmonious connections between humans, nature, and architecture, crafting meaningful and enduring spaces. Explore my portfolio to see how I bring these principles to life in every project.

I am currently involved in mentoring architecture students at Pars University, assisting in the Architectural Design 2 course. The course challenges students to design an art school inspired by contextual architecture, fostering a sense of place attachment, vitality, and the spirit of place. In parallel, I’ve embarked on an exciting journey by registering for an international architectural competition. Additionally, I am working on conceptualizing a tourism village near Tehran, blending cultural richness with innovative design.
